# Research scope

## Phase 0 — Understand the data

1. Inventory files and metadata.
2. Identify recording format, sampling rate, units and channel/electrode mapping.
3. Plot short windows of raw activity.
4. Measure missing data, clipping, drift and noise.
5. Determine whether spike timestamps are supplied or need to be detected.
6. Create a reproducible baseline analysis.

## Phase 1 — Activity features

- spike/event counts;
- firing rates;
- inter-spike intervals;
- burst statistics;
- channel correlations;
- synchronisation metrics;
- spectral and time-frequency summaries where appropriate.

## Phase 2 — Representation and modelling

- clustering recurring activity regimes;
- anomaly detection;
- dimensionality reduction;
- supervised/unsupervised models where labels or experimental events permit;
- experiment-to-experiment comparison.

## Non-goals at this stage

- culturing biological tissue;
- wet-lab experimentation;
- clinical or diagnostic interpretation;
- claims about cognition, consciousness or intelligence unsupported by the data.
